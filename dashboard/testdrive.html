Read this before you click

  • Sepolia testnet only. Squeekchain's SQK token here has no mainnet value. The exact call is approve(stakingProxy, 0) on the SQK token contract.
  • It sets the staking contract's existing allowance for your account to zero. It cannot transfer tokens, cannot grant an allowance, and does not touch any other contract.
  • Your wallet builds and shows the transaction for review first. You can reject it. Nothing is signed or sent without your confirmation.
  • Squeekwatch does not ask for and cannot read your private key, seed phrase, recovery code or password. There is no backend: the page is static files plus your own wallet.
  • This button stays disabled until your wallet is connected, on chainId 11155111, and you tick the box below.
